About DeckStation

An independent Bali workshop built around one idea: club-grade equipment should be serviced by people who actually use it, and repaired without shortcuts.

DeckStation Bali is an independent service station specialized in DJ and pro audio equipment repair and maintenance. We provide fast, precise, and trusted technical solutions for DJs, nightclubs, beach clubs, event organizers, and equipment rental providers across Bali.

Every repair is handled directly by our technicians — no outsourcing to third-party workshops — so the person who diagnoses your fault is the same person who performs the work and signs off the final test. We work on players, mixers, turntables, headphones, microphones and PA systems, either at our workshop or on-site at your venue.

Our Process

How a job moves through the workshop

Five checkpoints, from the first message to the final handover.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    You describe the fault and send photos or video. We reply within 24 hours with a likely cause and a work plan.

  2. 02

    Pickup or On-Site Visit

    We collect your equipment from your location anywhere in Bali, or meet you at the club, villa or studio for on-site work.

  3. 03

    Diagnosis & Quotation

    Full fault finding with a written scope: what is replaced, what is repaired, and the final cost — approved before work continues.

  4. 04

    Repair & Quality Control

    Repair using quality parts, then a full function and audio test: playback, cue, faders, outputs, level matching and firmware state.

  5. 05

    Handover & Aftercare

    Delivery with warranty documentation and a short handover briefing, plus aftercare support if anything feels off later.
