Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What equipment can you service? ▼
We handle CDJ players, DJM mixers, Technics SL-1200 series turntables, and various other DJ & pro audio equipment. If in doubt, contact us for a consultation.
How does the pickup system work? ▼
We are a call-out business. Simply fill out the booking form with your address, and we will pick up your equipment from your location across all Bali area. Once finished, we deliver it back to you.
How long does the repair take? ▼
Turnaround time varies depending on the issue. Standard services average 1-3 working days. For complex repairs (such as mainboard issues), it may take 3-7 days. We will provide an estimate after diagnosis.
Do you offer a warranty on repairs? ▼
Yes, every repair service comes with a warranty covering the components we worked on. Warranty details will be explained upon equipment return.
How much does service cost? ▼
Cost depends on the type of damage and required spare parts. We provide a cost estimate after diagnosis and before any work begins. You will receive a transparent cost breakdown.
How do I book a service? ▼
You can book through the Booking page on this website, or directly chat via WhatsApp to our number. After booking, we will confirm the pickup schedule within 24 hours.
Do you accept emergency repairs for events? ▼
Absolutely! We understand equipment can fail during event preparation. Contact us via WhatsApp and we will do our best to help as quickly as possible.
