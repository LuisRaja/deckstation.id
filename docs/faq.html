Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about our process, pricing, pickup and warranty. If your question is not listed, just ask us on WhatsApp.

Service & Scope
What equipment can you service?
We handle CDJ players (CDJ-1000, 2000, 3000), DJM mixers (DJM-600/800/900/900NXS2), Technics SL-1200 and SL-1210 turntables, other DJ gear from Denon, Numark, Stanton and Allen & Heath, plus headphones, audio interfaces, microphones and PA systems. If in doubt, contact us for a consultation.
What services do you offer?
Five service lines: all-in equipment repair (CDJ, DJM, amplifiers, DSP and equalizers), club & venue audio maintenance, audio system installation, DJ gear & event rental (players, mixers, speakers, lighting and laser), and pro audio consultation. See the Services page for the full list.
Can you service equipment that is still under warranty?
We can, but you should check with the manufacturer first. Most brands require authorised service for warranty claims. We are an independent workshop, so claims must be made directly with the manufacturer, while we can still handle the diagnostic and repair work.
How much does service cost?
Cost depends on the type of damage and the required spare parts. We provide a cost estimate after diagnosis and before any work begins, with a transparent breakdown of parts and labour. Diagnosis and consultation are free of charge.
Do you supply spare parts?
Yes. We supply original and compatible spare parts — tactile switches, faders, jog sensors, belts, cables, tonearms, capacitors and more. We can also order parts for rental fleets and advise which option fits your budget best.
Booking & Pickup
How does the pickup system work?
We are a call-out business. Fill out the booking form with your address and we will pick up your equipment from your location across all Bali area. Once finished, we deliver it back to you — or hand it over on-site if you prefer.
How do I book a service?
Use the Booking page on this website, or chat directly on WhatsApp. After booking we confirm the pickup schedule within 24 hours, then send a written scope and estimate before work starts.
Which areas in Bali do you cover?
We cover all of Bali, including Denpasar, Badung, Gianyar, Tabanan, Klungkung, Bangli and Jembrana. For venues, we can also perform the service on-site at your club, bar, beach club, villa or studio.
Do you accept emergency repairs for events?
Absolutely. We understand equipment can fail during event preparation. Contact us via WhatsApp with your deadline and we will prioritise the job, and where possible arrange a same-day pickup.
Turnaround & Warranty
How long does the repair take?
Turnaround time varies depending on the issue. Standard services average 1–3 working days. For complex repairs such as mainboard or display work, it may take 3–7 days, and longer if parts need to be ordered. We will provide a firm estimate after diagnosis.
Do you offer a warranty on repairs?
Yes, every repair service comes with a warranty covering the components we worked on. Warranty details are explained upon equipment return, and the same fault within the warranty period is re-checked at no extra charge.
What should I do before sending my equipment?
Note the brand, model and serial number of each unit, and send us a short video or photos of the fault so we can pre-diagnose. Pack accessories (power cables, flight case, link cables) together with the unit to avoid missing parts during the return.
On-Site & Payment
Can you come to my venue for maintenance?
Yes. We offer on-site maintenance packages for clubs, bars, beach clubs and rental providers — periodic check-ups, preventive cleaning, technical preparation before events and a written equipment condition report for your records.
How can I pay?
Payment can be made by bank transfer (BCA, BRI, Mandiri, BNI) or in cash when the unit is collected or delivered. For rental and venue accounts, we can issue a proper invoice and work on a monthly retainer arrangement.
Are spare parts genuine?
We use original manufacturer parts where available and quality compatible parts otherwise, and we always tell you which one was installed. Every replaced component is documented in the handover notes.
