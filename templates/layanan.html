How It Works

4 steps from problem to playtime

A clear, documented workflow so you always know what happens to your equipment.

  1. 01

    Send the details

    WhatsApp us the model, serial and symptoms — or fill the booking form. Photos and videos help us pre-diagnose for free.

  2. 02

    Diagnosis & estimate

    We confirm the fault, quote the scope and turnaround time before touching the unit. No surprise costs after the job starts.

  3. 03

    Repair & test

    Repair is done at the workshop with genuine parts, followed by full function testing and audio quality verification.

  4. 04

    Delivery & warranty

    Pickup or on-site handover, warranty documentation, plus a short handover briefing so the rig behaves exactly as expected.
