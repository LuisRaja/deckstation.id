About DeckStation

Get to know us

DeckStation Bali is an independent service station specialized in DJ and pro audio equipment repair and maintenance. We provide fast, precise, and trusted technical solutions for DJs, nightclubs, beach clubs, event organizers, and equipment rental providers across Bali.

Our Services

  • General Service & Cleaning: Thorough cleaning of mechanical & internal electronic components from dust, corrosion, and liquid residue.
  • Hardware Repair & Replacement: Tactile switch replacement (Cue/Play buttons), fader/crossfader, jog wheel repair, up to mainboard repair.
  • Turntables Calibration: Precision service, pitch control calibration, tonearm repair, and electrical work for Technics SL-1200 series.
  • On-Site Maintenance: Maintenance service directly at your venue for event preparation or routine club maintenance.

Why Choose DeckStation Bali?

  • ⚙️ Handled directly by experienced pro audio technicians.
  • 🛠️ Neat, precise work with transparent component/sparepart usage.
  • ⚡ Efficient turnaround to support your event schedule.
  • 🤝 Customer satisfaction and service warranty guaranteed.
